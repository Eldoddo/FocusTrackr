# FocusTrackr

AI assisted life prioritisation and productivity tool to help users make the most of their time. Creating an area to bring all tasks, commitments, and goals across work, family, personal responsibilities, and hobbies into one place. The tool will intelligently prioritise activities based on individual needs and competing demands, helping users focus on what matters most, reduce the mental burden of managing multiple responsibilities, and achieve a better balance between productivity and personal wellbeing.
